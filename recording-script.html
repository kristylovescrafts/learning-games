Sentence recording script

128 sentences — one for every word in Word Quest. Record only the sets you want to use.

How to do it
  1. Open Voice Memos and record yourself reading one sentence.
  2. Rename the recording to the filename in the middle column — all lower case, e.g. because.m4a
  3. Upload them to a folder called sentence-audio in your repo.
  4. Read at a normal pace with a small pause at the start. Say the sentence once only.
